Do LLMs actually feel emotions?

Short answer

We make no claim about feelings. We measure what the models do.

  • “Recognition” means correctly labelling the emotion in the partner’s message when asked
  • Our results are behavioural: how decisions change at fixed payoffs
  • We looked at no model internals and no brain data, so the neural parallels are analogies only
  • Justifications and reasoning text show what models report, not necessarily what drove the choice
