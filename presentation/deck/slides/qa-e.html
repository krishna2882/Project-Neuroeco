Did the models understand the games?

Short answer

Mostly yes. We asked them directly, in a pre-registered set of comprehension questions.

  • Llama answers 100% of the ultimatum payoff questions correctly, so its acceptance of unfair offers is a preference, not confusion
  • Qwen 7B and 14B: at least 95% correct on every question
  • Llama (17%) and Qwen-3B (29%) wrongly think Player B must send money back, so their trust results carry that caveat
  • Emotion recognition: 98.8–100% in every model
