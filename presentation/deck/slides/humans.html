Is the trust effect really about threat? Probably not (exploratory)

Across all 10 anger messages, the trust drop does not grow with the threat rating: −1.4 pp per +10 points of threat [−4.0, +1.3], p = .32. The least threatening message (A2, 11% of raters) still lowers trust by 11.7 pp on average.

Careful with the human comparison: raters answered a hypothetical giving question, not a trust decision, so “people give more, models trust less” is a contrast, not a finding. Dropping A3 and A4 weakens Qwen’s main-study anger effect to −6.6 pp [−12.8, −0.5], because only three anger messages remain and one of them (A7) has almost no effect. A pre-registered test with non-threatening anger was frozen but not run.
