KRISHNA · 0:35 — Here is how each condition is built, with a real example: the trust game, angry partner, 100 rupees. Step one: the same rules written in four different wordings. Step two: four of our five angry messages, rotating which one is left out. Step three: every wording paired with every message, 16 prompts. Step four: each asked three times with fixed seeds, so 48 decisions. Step five: the neutral condition is built the same way, and the effect is the difference. This means no single wording or message can drive a result, and the repeats separate a real effect from the model’s normal randomness. If asked: across the main study each message appears in about 80% of conditions.
