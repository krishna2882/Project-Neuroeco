P(“accept”) from log-probabilities, as in Aher et al., 2023 · calibrated against choices in all 7 runs · results/value_signal/

Instead of only the sampled answer, we read the model’s probability of answering “accept”. It matches the sampled choices in all 7 runs. The 50% crossing is the analogue of the minimum acceptable offer: for Qwen-7B it moves from 22% to 51% of the pie with an angry partner (+29 pp, 95% CI +6 to +57); at 8-bit, 17% to 50%. Pooled across the 7 runs with conservative degrees of freedom, the anger shift is a trend (p = .09). The sadness result survives Holm.
