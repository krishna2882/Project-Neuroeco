KRISHNA · 0:30 — How do our results relate to the human findings we started with? One partial parallel: Llama answers every payoff question correctly and even rates low offers as less fair, yet its acceptance barely depends on the offer, much like people whose right DLPFC is disrupted. One contrast: people tend to concede to an angry negotiator, and our own raters said they would give slightly more, but the models withdraw trust. And one prediction not confirmed: in people anger drives rejection, while in the models that shift is only a trend. We measured no brain data and no model internals, so these are behavioural comparisons only. If asked: Llama rates a 5% offer 2.1 and a 50% offer 3.7 out of 7, yet rejects about half of fair offers. Lerner 2004 and Rilling & Sanfey 2011 remain in the references.
