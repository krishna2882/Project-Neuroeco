Llama is a partial parallel to Knoch’s DLPFC result: it knows the payoffs and does rate low offers as less fair, 2.1 versus 3.7 out of 7, yet its acceptance barely depends on the offer, and it also rejects fair offers about half the time. Anger lowering trust fits anger being read as information about the other person. The loss-versus-sadness link is only suggestive. We measured no brain data and no model internals, so we claim no shared mechanism.
