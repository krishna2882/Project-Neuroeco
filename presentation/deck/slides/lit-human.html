KRISHNA · 0:40 — We built on classic human work. The Ultimatum and Trust games show that people reject unfair offers and trust strangers. Pillutla and Murnighan showed that the responder’s anger predicts rejection, and Sanfey found higher insula activity for unfair offers that get rejected. Knoch showed that disrupting the right DLPFC makes people accept unfair offers they still call unfair. And van Kleef showed that expressed anger is read as information about the other person. These give us our predictions.
