The question

If the other player sounds angry, sad or happy, does the AI decide differently, even when the money is identical?

Why it matters

LLMs are used as negotiating agents and as “simulated participants” in social-science studies. If an emotional message moves their choices, that is a lever, and a bias.

What neuroeconomics predicts

In people, emotion changes fairness and trust decisions: anger drives rejection of unfair offers, and expressed anger is read as information about the other person.

Our test

Hold payoffs fixed. Change only the counterpart’s short message: neutral, angry, sad, happy, or none. Measure the decision.
