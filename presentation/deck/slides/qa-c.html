Is it anger, or perceived threat?

Short answer

Threat does not explain it in our data, but with 10 anger messages we cannot rule it out.

  • 12 raters flagged 4 of the 10 anger messages as threatening; without them the pre-registered effect holds: −11.4 pp, Holm p = .021
  • Threat ratings do not predict the size of the drop: −1.3 pp per +10 points of threat, p = .43
  • The least threatening message, A2 (8% of raters), still cuts trust by about 20 pp in Llama and Qwen-14B
  • A test with deliberately non-threatening anger is pre-registered and ready to run
