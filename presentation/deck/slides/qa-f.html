Were analyses chosen after seeing the data?

Short answer

Our claims rest on tests fixed in advance. Everything added later is labelled exploratory.

  • Confirmatory tests F1, F2 and X1–X3 were frozen with a SHA-256 hash and pushed to GitHub before any data
  • Holm correction across all 42 planned tests in the whole study
  • Later analyses (random-factor refit, equivalence, valuation curves, wording) are labelled post hoc
  • 21 deviations from the plan are logged; the follow-up’s second sign-off came after data and is disclosed
