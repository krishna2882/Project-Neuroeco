Takeaways

1 · The models recognise a partner’s emotion almost perfectly, but it rarely changes what they do.

2 · The exception: an angry partner gets less trust, replicated in four pre-registered tests.

3 · How the game is worded moves decisions more than emotion does.

Code, data, pre-registrations and every deviation: github.com/krishna2882/Project-Neuroeco

Also worth saying if asked: the sadness effect was a reaction to the described loss, not to sadness; Mistral, the large reasoning model, shows its emotion sensitivity as generosity. Claims cover open models up to 14B plus Mistral Large 4.
