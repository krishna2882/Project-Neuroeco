Is the anger effect real, or noise?

Short answer

Real within the Qwen family: it survived every test we designed to break it. Llama goes the same way; Mistral shows nothing.

  • 4 pre-registered tests replicated: new messages (F2) and Qwen 3B, 14B and 8-bit (X1–X3)
  • Pooled Qwen-7B: −10.1 pp [−14.4, −5.8]; the two studies agree (p = .74)
  • Message treated as a random factor, so the interval includes message-to-message variation
  • Unchanged at temperature 0.7 (−11.5 pp) and without threat-flagged messages (−11.4 pp)
