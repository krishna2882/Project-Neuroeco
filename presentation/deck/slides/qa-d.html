Why these models, not GPT or Claude?

Short answer

Budget and control. Open-weight models let us read probabilities, fix seeds and re-run everything identically.

  • Open models ran free on the university GPU cluster; commercial APIs were outside the budget
  • Open weights give token log-probabilities and exact reproducibility
  • Coverage: one family at 3B, 7B and 14B, a second family (Llama), and one large reasoning model (Mistral Large 4)
  • The pipeline is model-agnostic: a frontier model needs only an API key and credits
