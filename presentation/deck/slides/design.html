KRISHNA · 0:45 — The model plays four roles: accepting or rejecting an ultimatum offer, proposing a split with and without the partner’s veto, and both sides of the trust game, where money sent is tripled. Before deciding, the partner sends a message: neutral, angry, sad or happy, five messages each, or none. Every prompt comes in four wordings and with four different messages, three samples each, so no single wording or message drives a result.
