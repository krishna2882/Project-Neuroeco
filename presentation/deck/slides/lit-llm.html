KRISHNA · 0:35 — On the AI side, Horton, Aher and Mei showed that LLMs can stand in for economic agents, and Aher read acceptance directly from the model’s token probabilities, a method we use later. The closest work, Mozikov and colleagues, puts the emotion into the model itself. The gap: nobody had held payoffs fixed and varied only the emotion the other player expresses, across Ultimatum, Dictator and Trust games, with pre-registered replications.
