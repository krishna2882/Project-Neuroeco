DEVANSH · 0:30 — We also coded what the models write to justify their decisions, both of us independently and blind, with agreement between 0.79 and 0.87. They mention the emotion when there is one: Qwen does so 41 percent of the time after an emotional message versus 4 percent otherwise. Llama mentions fairness in 76 percent of its rejections. And compared with each other, Llama mentions emotion most, Qwen fairness most, and Mistral strategy most. If asked: justifications come after the decision, so they show what the models report, not what drove the choice.
