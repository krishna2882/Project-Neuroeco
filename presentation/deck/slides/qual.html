Each model has its own style: Llama mentions emotion most, Qwen reasons about fairness, Mistral about strategy. Strategy mentions are not higher in the ultimatum than in the dictator game (41% vs 53%, p = .44), consistent with no strategic use of anger. Justifications come after the decision, so they show what the models report, not what drove the choice. Exploratory; Fisher’s exact tests with Holm correction.
