DEVANSH · 0:30 — Here is what that looks like for one message. A2 is clearly angry, but only one of our twelve raters read it as a threat, and people would not punish it: a quarter would give less, a third would give more. The models react very differently. Llama and Qwen-14B cut the share they send by about 20 points, and their own justifications say why: an angry partner looks unlikely to pay back. Qwen-7B cuts less, and Mistral, which sends almost nothing anyway, does not change. If asked: raters answered a giving question, not the trust game, so this is a contrast, not a direct comparison. Quotes are from the 20% of trials that asked for a justification.
