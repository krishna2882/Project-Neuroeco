Can 5 messages per emotion generalise?

Short answer

It is our main limit, and we built the analysis around it rather than ignoring it.

  • The statistics treat the message as a random factor, so results are meant to hold for other messages, with wider intervals
  • The follow-up used 5 completely new messages per emotion: anger replicated, sadness did not
  • Between-message variation is estimated and reported for every effect; it is often large (A7 has almost no anger effect)
  • More messages per emotion is the clearest next step for future work
