Does it generalise beyond our messages?

Short answer

We designed for this from the start, and the anger effect passed every check we could run.

  • The message is a random factor in every model, so our intervals already account for which messages were used
  • Replicated with 5 completely new, event-free anger messages (F2)
  • Holds without the 4 messages raters found threatening (−11.4 pp [−20.0, −2.9]), and threat ratings do not predict its size (p = .43)
  • Even the least threatening message, A2, cuts trust by about 20 pp in Llama and Qwen-14B
  • Ready next: more messages per emotion, and a pre-registered test with non-threatening anger
